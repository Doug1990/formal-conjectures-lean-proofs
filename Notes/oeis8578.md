# OeisA8578.conjecture

- Source: `FormalConjectures/OEIS/8578.lean:65` (category research solved)
- Statement: `A k ↔ k * cardFactors k < sigma 1 k`, where `A k := k = 1 ∨ k.Prime`.

Proof idea: k = 0, 1 and primes are direct. For Ω(k) ≥ 2 we show σ(k) ≤ k·Ω(k) by strong
induction. Write k = P·m with P the largest prime factor. Lemma `sigma_le`, by induction on Q,
gives σ(m) ≤ (Q−1)·m whenever all prime factors of m are < Q. It splits off Q^e and uses
(Q−1)·σ(Q^e) < Q^(e+1). With Q = P+1 this gives σ(m) ≤ P·m. Lemma `sigma_prime_mul` gives
σ(P·m) ≤ (P+1)·σ(m) (splitting m = P^e·r coprime). If Ω(m) ≥ 2, the IH gives
σ(k) ≤ P·m·Ω(m) + P·m = k·Ω(k). If m = q is prime with q ≤ P, then (P+1)(q+1) ≤ 2Pq for
P ≥ 3, and the remaining case k = 4 is σ(4) = 7 ≤ 8.
