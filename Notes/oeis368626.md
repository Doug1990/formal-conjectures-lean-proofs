# OeisA368626.conjecture

- Source: FormalConjectures/OEIS/368626.lean:99 (category research solved)
- Statement: `Odd (a n) ↔ ∃ k : ℕ, n = 2 ^ k - 1`

Proof idea: the argument works modulo 2 (in `ZMod 2`). The sum conv2 a N is symmetric under k ↦ N−k. Pairs cancel through an involution (`Finset.sum_involution`), so conv2 a N ≡ [N even]·a(N/2)² ≡ [N even]·a(N/2). This is the coefficient form of A² ≡ A(x²). As a result a(n) is even for every even n ≥ 2. For odd n = 2m+1, conv3 a (2m) = Σ conv2 a k · a(2m−k) ≡ Σ_{k even} a(k/2)·a(2m−k). Only k = 2m survives, because a(2m−k) is even otherwise. So a(2m+1) ≡ a(m). Strong induction then gives the claim, with a(0) = 1 as the base case.
