# OeisA368633.conjecture_1

- Source: FormalConjectures/OEIS/368633.lean:88 (category research solved)
- Statement: `Odd (a n) ↔ ∃ k, n + 1 = 2 ^ k` for the coefficients of A(x)=1+2xA(x)²−xA(−x)².
- Proof idea: modulo 2 the factor 3 is 1, so a(n) ≡ conv2 a (n−1). A symmetric convolution square collapses mod 2
  (involution k ↦ N−k) to [N even]·a(N/2). Hence a(2j+2) ≡ 0 and a(2m+1) ≡ a(m); strong induction on n
  with 2^(k+1) = 2·2^k finishes. Same template as results/oeis368629.lean.
