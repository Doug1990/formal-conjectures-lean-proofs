# OeisA368692.a_is_int

- Source: `FormalConjectures/OEIS/368692.lean:76` (category research solved)
- Statement: `108 * ((4n+2)! * (2n+3)! * ((6n+5)!)^2) ∣ (12n+6)! * (6n+9)!`

**Proof idea.** Put m = 2n+1. Cancelling (6n+5)!·9(2n+2)(2n+3) from both sides reduces the claim to
`Q.B`: 12·(2m)!·m!·(3m+2)! ∣ (6m)!·(3m+4)(3m+5) for odd m. That is proved one prime at a time with Legendre's formula.
For every q ≥ 2 (with m odd when q = 2), the floor inequality ⌊2m/q⌋+⌊m/q⌋+⌊(3m+2)/q⌋ ≤ ⌊6m/q⌋ holds (`Q.layer`, reduced to m mod q, then case split by interval_cases and omega). So the 3 in 12 and the 4 in 12 need extra room.
For p = 3, the layer q = 3^(log₃ m + 1) is strict (`Q.top3`).
For p = 2, 3m+5 is even. If 4 ∣ 3m+1 then 4 ∣ 3m+5. Otherwise the layer q = 2^(log₂ m + 1) ≥ 4 is strict (`Q.top2`, which uses m odd and 4 ∤ 3m+1).
