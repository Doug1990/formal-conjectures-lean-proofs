- Statement: a(n) = ⌊Σ_{k=1}^n {n/k}⌋ satisfies a(n) ~ (1 − γ)·n.

## Proof idea
Σ_{k≤n} {n/k} = n·H_n − Σ_{k≤n} ⌊n/k⌋ = n·H_n − D(n), where D(n) = Σ_{m≤n} τ(m) is the divisor summatory function. This uses ⌊n/k⌋ = n div k and the divisor-swap identity. Two earlier results bound the error terms, and both are reused verbatim from results/divisor_dirichlet.lean: Dirichlet's hyperbola method gives |D(n) − n log n − (2γ−1)n| ≤ 10√n (turned into the explicit lemma `err_bound`), and |H_n − log n − γ| ≤ 1/n. Together with the floor they give |a(n) − (1−γ)n| ≤ 12√n for n ≥ 1. Since γ < 2/3 < 1, the little-o condition follows directly from `isLittleO_iff`: for n ≥ (12/(c(1−γ)))², 12√n ≤ c(1−γ)n.
