# OeisA93456.conjecture

- Source: FormalConjectures/OEIS/93456.lean:64 (category research solved)
- Statement: `¬ Set.Finite {n : ℕ | 1 < n ∧ ¬ (a (n - 1) ∣ a n)}`, where a(n) is the product of the composites in [C(n,2)+1, C(n+1,2)].

Proof idea: take a large prime p (p ≥ C(N+1,2)+20) and the block index m with C(m,2) < 2p ≤ C(m+1,2), found with Nat.find. The composite 2p lies in block m, so p ∣ a(m). Since C(m,2) < 2p forces 2m+1 ≤ p (via C(m,2) ≥ 4m for m ≥ 9), block m+1 lies strictly between 2p and 3p, so it contains no multiple of p. Since p is prime, p ∤ a(m+1), and therefore a(m) ∤ a(m+1). The choice of p gives m > N, so the set is unbounded.
