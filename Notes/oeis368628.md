- Statement: `Odd (a n) ↔ ∃ k : ℕ, n = (4 ^ k - 1) / 3`

Proof idea: as in A368626, everything is computed in `ZMod 2`. For any f, the symmetric sum conv2 f N collapses through an involution to [N even]·f(N/2). This is A² ≡ A(x²) mod 2. Applying it twice to conv4 = conv2 ∘ conv2 gives a(2m+1) ≡ [m even]·a(m/2), and conv2 alone gives a(even ≥ 2) ≡ 0. So a(4j+1) ≡ a(j), and a(4j+2), a(4j+3) and a(4j) for j ≥ 1 are all even. The numbers s_k = (4^k−1)/3 satisfy s_{k+1} = 4 s_k + 1 (since 4^k ≡ 1 mod 3), so strong induction over n mod 4 closes the proof.
