# OeisA113255.conjecture

- Source: FormalConjectures/OEIS/113255.lean:70 (category research solved; an external formal proof exists)
- Statement: for all n, a(2n+1) is a perfect square, where a(n+4) = -4a(n+3) + 324a(n+1) + 6561a(n) and a(0..3) = -1, 4, 227, 5329 (the m = 9 member of the A113249 family).

Proof idea: let u(0)=2, u(1)=73, u(n+2) = -4u(n+1) - 81u(n). (x²-4x+81)(x⁴+4x³-324x-6561) = x⁶+65x⁴-5265x²-531441, so a satisfies a(n+6) = -65a(n+4) + 5265a(n+2) + 531441a(n) (`linear_combination r2 - 4r1 + 81r0`). u² satisfies the same recurrence (`ring`). Induction on three consecutive odd indices then gives a(2n+1) = u(n)².
