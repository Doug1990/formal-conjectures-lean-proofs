# OeisA3625.conjecture

- Source: `FormalConjectures/OEIS/3625.lean:79` (category research solved)
- Statement: for a prime p, p ≡ 3, 5, 6 (mod 7) ⟺ X² + X + 2 is irreducible over ZMod p.

**Proof idea.** A quadratic over a field is irreducible iff it has no root (`irreducible_iff_roots_eq_zero_of_degree_le_three`). p = 2 and p = 7 are handled by explicit roots (0 and 3). For other p, 2 is invertible and 4(x²+x+2) = (2x+1)² + 7, so a root exists iff −7 is a square mod p. By `legendreSym.mul`, `at_neg_one` and `quadratic_reciprocity'` we get (−7/p) = χ₄(p)·(−1)^(3·(p/2))·(p/7) = (p/7). Finally (p/7) = 1 iff p mod 7 ∈ {1, 2, 4}, checked by `decide` over ZMod 7.

Axioms: `propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound`
