- Proof idea: Unfold the `Nat.strongRecOn` definition (it reduces to `WellFounded.fix`, so `WellFounded.fix_eq`) to get D(m+1) = Σ_{i≤m} c(m+1−i)·D(i). Introduce Y(m) = Σ_{i<m} a·b^(m−1−i)·D(i). Splitting off the last term gives D(m+1) = D(m) + Y(m) and Y(m+1) = a·D(m) + b·Y(m). These are exactly the recurrences for the first row of M^n with M = [[1,a],[1,b]] (M^(n+1) = M^n·M). Induction on n then shows (M^n)₀₀ = D(n) and (M^n)₀₁ = Y(n).
