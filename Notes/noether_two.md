## Proof idea
Let x_i = e⁻¹(X_i). Every K-intermediate field of L containing all x_i is ⊤: it contains the images of all polynomials, and every element of the fraction field is a quotient of two of them. So K-automorphisms of L are determined by their values on the x_i. If H is trivial, its fixed field is ⊤ ≅ L. Otherwise H contains some τ = conj(σ) ≠ 1, and σ has to be the swap of the two indices a, b. Then τ² = 1, so orderOf τ = 2, and Artin's theorem (`FixedPoints.finrank_eq_card`) gives [L : L^⟨τ⟩] = 2. Let S = K(e₁, e₂) be generated by the elementary symmetric polynomials, i.e. S = K(x+y, xy). Then S ≤ L^H ≤ L^⟨τ⟩. Also L = S(x), and x is a root of T² − (x+y)T + xy, so [L : S] ≤ 2. `eq_of_le_of_finrank_le'` gives S = L^⟨τ⟩, which squeezes L^H = S. Finally, e₁ and e₂ are algebraically independent (`esymmAlgHom_injective`, the fundamental theorem of symmetric polynomials). So `AlgebraicIndependent.aevalEquivField` gives S ≅ K(T₁, T₂), with ι' = Fin 2.
