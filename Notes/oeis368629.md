# OeisA368629.conjecture

- Source: `FormalConjectures/OEIS/368629.lean:99` (category research solved)
- Statement: `Odd (a n) ↔ ∃ k, n = 2 ^ k - 1`

Proof idea: work mod 2. A symmetric convolution sum Σ_k f(k) f(N−k) collapses (involution k ↦ N−k) to
[N even]·f(N/2)² = [N even]·f(N/2) in ZMod 2. For even n ≥ 2, a(n) = conv4 a (n−1) with n−1 odd, so the
outer conv2 vanishes mod 2. For odd n = 2m+1, a(n) = conv2 a (2m) ≡ a(m). Strong induction plus
2^(k+1) − 1 = 2(2^k − 1) + 1 gives the characterisation.

Axioms: `propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound`
