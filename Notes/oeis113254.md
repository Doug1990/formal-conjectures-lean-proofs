# OeisA113254.a_odd_is_square

- Source: FormalConjectures/OEIS/113254.lean:79 (category research solved; an external formal proof exists)
- Statement: for all n, a(2n+1) is a perfect square, where a(n+4) = -4a(n+3) + 256a(n+1) + 4096a(n) and a(0..3) = -1, 4, 176, 3136 (the m = 8 member of the A113249 family).

Proof idea: let u(0)=2, u(1)=56, u(n+2) = -4u(n+1) - 64u(n). Multiplying the characteristic polynomial x⁴+4x³-4m²x-m⁴ by x²-4x+m² gives an even polynomial, so a satisfies the order-6 recurrence a(n+6) = -48a(n+4) + 3072a(n+2) + 262144a(n) (`linear_combination r2 - 4r1 + 64r0`). u² satisfies the same 3-term recurrence (`ring`). Induction on three consecutive odd indices then gives a(2n+1) = u(n)².
